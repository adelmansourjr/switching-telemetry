# Frozen protocol for two simultaneous counter faults

## Estimand and deductions

The target is joint duty `p11` over an abstract two-relay window. Both displacement observations stay within their advertised error radii in the primary experiment. Five distinct counters from the existing six-counter menu are read once. Up to two counter reports may take arbitrary values. A reading is compatible when one simplex vector satisfies both displacement constraints and at least three of the five counter constraints.

On each shared transcript compare three sound estimators. The **exact** interval is the hull of the projections of all feasible three-counter support polytopes. The primary baseline is the **pairwise envelope**. For each three-counter support, intersect the three two-counter projected intervals before taking the hull over supports. This allows different occupancy vectors behind the pairwise intervals, while the exact analysis requires one vector for all three. The simpler **median** uses the median lower and upper endpoints from the five individual-counter intervals. Empty supports contribute lower endpoint plus infinity and upper endpoint minus infinity. The same nominally calibrated displacement bands enter all three methods. Check that the exact interval is contained in both sound baselines. A fourth, deliberately misspecified control uses the exact interval under an **at-most-one-fault** assumption on the same five readings.

Two compatible occupancy vectors must have at least one common sound counter because their support sets each have size at least three. Conversely, if their displacement error boxes and any one counter error interval overlap, construct a common transcript with common midpoints and two counter reports from each vector among the four remaining channels. Thus the minimax width of a five-counter design is the largest single-counter diameter for those counters with displacement present. Freeze the design before reading values by excluding the counter with highest single-counter diameter, equivalently selecting the first menu-ordered five-counter subset that minimises the maximum diameter rounded to 11 decimal places. Recheck the diameter on the final transcripts. The minimax result is for the occupancy simplex. Its attaining populations need not be attainable under a shared relay law.

## Endpoints and audit

The sole primary endpoint is the paired difference between the pairwise envelope and the exact decoder on worst two-fault-probe width, averaged across trajectories within each field and then across fields. A primary success requires zero uncovered, empty or wrong decisions for both estimators, a mean improvement of at least .005 absolute and 10% relative, and a positive lower endpoint from the two-sided percentile 95% interval. Bootstrap whole fields 4,000 times with NumPy seed 7848912. Worst width excludes the clean case. The practical gate is an exploratory study-design choice carried over from the one-fault study.

Also report mean fault-probe width, clean width, coverage, empty intervals, correct and wrong threshold calls for all methods and both shift calibration states. Average width for an unsafe method uses only its valid intervals. If any fault probe is empty for a trajectory, do not compute that trajectory's worst width for the unsafe method. Thresholds remain .15, .25, .35, .45, and a declaration is made only when the interval lies strictly on one side. All threshold calls are counted across probes and thresholds, with field resampling only for the single primary contrast. Report under-budget failure even when an interval is nonempty. Store field-level paired widths and counts, fixed split seeds, and deterministic replay results. Use Python 3.12, NumPy 2.3.5 and single-thread BLAS. These are generated, paired stress tests under specified laws.
