# Certifying a target occupancy band

Given exactly the same two displacement measurements, five counter reports and two-fault allowance, compare the convex hull of feasible joint occupancies with the union of the projected intervals for all three-counter supports. An action is certified inside or outside a closed band only when every feasible occupancy agrees. Empty feasible sets are treated as abstentions. Both methods use a numerical guard of eight solver tolerances at each boundary.

Use fresh field seeds 890271 and 890281 with 48 nominal and 24 widened-calibration fields, respectively. Exclude the fields from the original, one-fault and two-fault panels. Noise and probe seeds are 637081 and 637111. Generate two settings per field, each with three relay kernels and the existing clean and paired-fault probes. Use the original five-counter design. In the widened-calibration panel, multiply the data-generating and assumed displacement radii by three and substitute the delayed kernel for sign. These are engineered stresses. Probe counts are neither independent failures nor a failure-rate estimate.

The primary outcome is the absolute percentage-point improvement in correct decisions across four prespecified 10-point-wide bands centred at 0.15, 0.25, 0.35 and 0.45. Require zero wrong decisions and at least a 1-point improvement over the exact hull for a practically meaningful nominal gain. Report band decisions by clean and fault probes, the number of disconnected feasible projections, and the paired 95% whole-field bootstrap interval. The widened panel is a stress check. The union must cover every true occupancy, and every exact-hull decision must be preserved.

Independently verify an exact rational fixture with five gates: mode-00, mode-10, first switch, second switch and XOR. Use report (1,0,0,1,0), zero counter error and displacement intervals wide enough to allow every occupancy. Verify every three-counter support by rational vertex enumeration. Check the union and its hull for the band [2/5, 3/5]. The fixture is for the observation model and does not purport to be a reachable calibrated relay pair.
